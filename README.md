One complete story in exactly six words. Free to enter, $5 pool, an AI judge ranks the stories and the top three places get paid.

## The story

> His last voicemail. She still listens.

Six words, 38 characters. A whole life implied: he is gone, his last message remains, and she still listens to it.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22) writes the story to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`, then verifies the gate: UTF-8 text, exactly six words, 1 to 60 characters, no empty words, no double spaces, no leading or trailing whitespace, byte-identical on read-back.

Run: `node main.ts` (with `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` set; falls back to `./output`).
